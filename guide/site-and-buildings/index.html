Site & structure

Site and buildings: reactor building, crane, truck, control room, casks

The reactor module arrives on one truck and sets into a concrete silo inside a steel building. A small control building, a used fuel cask pad, and a bridge crane complete the site.

Reactor building

A steel building encloses the reactor module and the power unit. Inside it, a concrete shield silo holds the module, gives seismic protection, and forms a final barrier for sodium.

Footprint≈ 18 × 12 m
Shield silo≈ 1.4 m reinforced concrete
StructureSteel frame, insulated panels
SeismicModule on isolated base

Bridge crane

An overhead bridge crane runs the length of the building. It lifts the reactor head, the shield plug, and the spent core cask during refueling.

Capacity≈ 40 t
Span≈ 12 m
HoistElectric wire-rope, two speeds
UseRefueling and major maintenance

Module delivery truck

A heavy-haul trailer brings a complete reactor module from the factory. The crane lifts the skid off the trailer and sets it in the silo.

LoadOne module on its skid, ≈ 80 t
TrailerLowboy, multi-axle
PermitsStandard oversize-load route
Factory to siteDays, not months

Control & operations building

Operators monitor the plant from this building. The reactor runs on its own, and the control system needs only oversight, so the room is small.

Staff on shift2 operators, 1 security
MonitoringLocal control room plus remote center
Also insideOffices, instrument shop, break room
Footprint≈ 14 × 6 m, two floors

Used fuel cask pad

After a core swap, the used core cools in a steel and concrete cask on this pad. The casks are passive, need no power, and ship off site later.

Casks4 positions
ShieldingSteel canister in a concrete overpack
CoolingPassive air, no power
Dose at fenceBelow public limits

Transport skid

The reactor module ships from the factory on a steel skid. The skid sets the module in the shield silo and acts as its seismic base.

FitsStandard heavy-haul trailer
Mass≈ 80 t with module
TransportTruck or rail
Install timeWeeks, not years

About

A representative design for education. Values are typical of a 10 MWe class pool-type sodium-cooled microreactor and of a data center of about 8 MW. They do not describe a specific product.
